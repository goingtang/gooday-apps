CareSync Privacy Policy

Effective: November 16, 2026　Last updated: November 25, 2026 (English version added)

Summary

  • CareSync needs no account and has no server of its own. Everything you enter stays on your phone, and we never receive it.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Only when you use Nearby pharmacies is the address you type, or your approximate location, sent to OpenStreetMap services to look up pharmacies.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the iOS and Android versions of CareSync (孝親合作, "the app"), provided by Gooday Creative (好日子創意所, "we").

2. Data we collect

We do not collect any of your personal data. The app needs no sign-up or login. It does not collect real names, phone numbers, email addresses, ID numbers or photos, and it uses no advertising identifiers, analytics or crash reporting services.

3. Data stored on your phone

What you enter in the app, including family nicknames, what you call the person you care for, blood pressure and blood sugar readings, medications, care log entries, schedules, tasks, expenses, family chat and shopping lists, is stored only in the app's own storage on your phone. It is protected by your phone's app sandbox and is never uploaded to us.

4. Health information

The app lets you record an older family member's blood pressure, blood sugar and medications. This information stays on your phone and is never uploaded, shared or used for any other purpose.

Reference values and reminders in the app are for information only and are not a medical diagnosis or advice. If the person you care for feels unwell, see a doctor or call your local emergency number.

5. Location and third-party services

The app asks for location permission only when you open Nearby pharmacies and tap My location. Your location is first rounded to about 100 meters, then sent directly to OpenStreetMap services (Nominatim and the Overpass API) to find pharmacies nearby. It is not stored afterwards. You can also search by typing an address without turning on location.

These services are run by third parties under their own privacy policies: OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy. Tapping Directions or Call opens the maps or phone app on your device.

6. Notifications

Reminders, such as the evening before your shift and when a task is due, are scheduled locally on your phone. They do not go over the internet or through any server of ours. You can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings.

7. Sharing

Only when you tap Share does the app open your phone's share sheet, where you choose which app to send to (for example, Messages or LINE). From then on, that app's policies apply.

7-1. Buying the full version

The full version is paid through the App Store. Payment is handled by Apple, and we do not receive your name, payment method or billing details. Your purchase status is stored only on your phone. On a new phone or after reinstalling, tap Restore purchases in Plans to get it back from the App Store.

8. Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can:

  • edit or delete any record in the app;
  • delete all data in More → Settings → Clear all;
  • uninstall the app, which also deletes its data from your phone.

Because we do not keep your data, we cannot restore anything you delete.

9. Children's privacy

The app is meant for adult family members caring for an older relative. It is not designed for children under 13 and does not collect anyone's personal data on our servers.

10. Changes to this policy

If we later add cloud sync, login or any other feature that sends data, we will update this page and the effective date above before the feature launches, and mention it in the app's release notes.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email goodaycreation@gmail.com.

If the English and Chinese versions differ, the Traditional Chinese version prevails.
