BucketGuard Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

繁體中文 · English

Summary

  • BucketGuard needs no account and has no server of its own. Your savings buckets, trials, subscriptions and amounts stay on your phone; we never receive them.
  • It does not connect to your bank and has no analytics of its own.
  • Unless you buy "Remove Ads", some tabs show a Google AdMob banner (section 5).

1. Scope

This policy covers BucketGuard (the "App") provided by Gooday Creative ("we", "us").

2. Data we do not collect

The App requires no sign-up and sends no name, email, phone number or other identifying data to any server. We run no analytics or crash reporting of our own. The only exception is the advertising service in section 5, which collects device and diagnostic data under its provider's policy.

3. Data stored on your phone

  • Bucket names, targets, target dates and saved amounts
  • Trial and subscription names, charge amounts and times, cancellation notes
  • Optional last four digits of a card (never the full number, expiry or security code)
  • Bank balances and salary you enter yourself (the App never connects to a bank)
  • Your records (deposits, payday splits, cancellations, spending) and display settings

This data is kept only in the App's folder on your phone (with iOS file protection). It is not uploaded to us and there is no cloud sync.

4. Permissions

PermissionUse
NotificationsLocal reminders before a free trial charges (48/24/12/6/2 hours), on payday and before subscription charges. Scheduled on the phone, no server, works offline.
Face ID / Touch ID (optional)Only when you turn on the privacy lock. The system checks it; the App never gets face or fingerprint data.
Tracking (App Tracking Transparency)Advertising only (section 5). Every feature works if you decline.

The App does not use contacts, camera, photos, microphone or precise location.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • Without "Remove Ads", one banner appears at the bottom of the Buckets, Payday and Insights tabs, starting 3 days after install.
  • The Trial Guard tab, its 24-hour alerts, Settings and all input screens never show ads.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect the device advertising identifier, approximate location (from the IP address), ad and product interaction data, and crash and performance diagnostics, to serve ads, prevent fraud and measure performance.
  • On iPhone an explanation appears first, then Apple asks whether to allow tracking. If you decline you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland first see Google's consent form and can change their choice in Settings → Privacy options (ads).
  • We never share your buckets, trials, subscriptions, accounts or any amounts with Google or any advertiser.
  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Remove Ads (one-time purchase)

"Remove Ads" is a one-time purchase handled by the App Store. We only learn whether your Apple ID owns it; we never receive card or payment details. After purchase the App no longer loads or shows ads.

7. Backups

Backups exported from Settings → Encrypted backup are encrypted with your password (XChaCha20-Poly1305, key derived with scrypt). You keep the file; we never receive it. The password is not stored, so a forgotten password cannot be recovered.

8. External websites

"Open official page" on a trial opens that service's website in your browser; its own policy then applies.

9. Deleting data

Use Settings → Clear all data, or delete the App. We hold none of your data, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from children.

11. Changes

We will update this page and the in-app effective date when this policy changes, and announce material changes in the App.
