Summary

  • PetPal needs no account and has no server of its own. Everything you enter stays on your phone; we never receive it.
  • No analytics of our own. Unless you buy “Remove ads”, a Google AdMob ad appears at the bottom of the screen; see section 5-1. (The map preview is drawn by Google's or Apple's map service, which collects data under its own policy; see section 5.)
  • Data goes to a third-party service only when you use a specific feature (Nearby places, Ask AI, sharing) and only as described below.

1. Scope

This policy covers the iOS and Android versions of PetPal ("the App"), provided by Gooday Creative (好日子創意所, "the Developer", "we").

2. Data we do not collect

The App requires no sign-up or log-in. It does not send your name, email, phone number or any other identifying data to any server, and it has no analytics or crash-reporting services of its own. The exceptions are the third-party map SDK described in section 5 and the ads described in section 5-1, which collect device and diagnostic data under its provider's policy.

3. Data stored on your phone

What you enter, including pet profiles, care logs, diary, health and medication records, reminders, expenses, contact phone numbers, photos and documents, is stored only in the App's folder on your phone. Photos are resized and re-saved (removing GPS and other camera metadata) before they are kept. None of this is uploaded to us.

3-1. How data is protected

  • Encrypted data file: pets, logs, diary, phone numbers and other text are stored encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305. The key is generated by the App and kept in the phone's secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore); it never leaves the phone and is never written to any file or backup.
  • Photos and documents: protected by the phone's file encryption.
  • App lock: on by default for new installs; opening the App asks for your fingerprint, face or phone passcode. Recognition is done by the phone; the App never sees your biometric data.
  • Encrypted backups: exporting a backup asks for a password by default and the backup file (.petpal) is encrypted with it. We do not have your password and cannot restore a backup without it.
  • Clipboard: after you copy information for a clinic, a health report, a care sheet or an AI question, the clipboard is cleared after 2 minutes (if you have not copied something else).
  • Lock screen: new installs show only "PetPal reminder" on the lock screen, without pet or medicine names.
  • Temporary files: PDFs, CSVs and backups made for sharing are deleted when they are more than 10 minutes old.

5. Data sent to third parties (only when you act)

  • Nearby places: your location is blurred to about 100 m and sent to public OpenStreetMap-based services (overpass-api.de, the volunteer-run mirror overpass.private.coffee, and nominatim.openstreetmap.org as fallbacks). They see the approximate location and your IP address under their own policies. The App does not store your location. Place data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Map preview: the small maps in Nearby places and directory details are drawn on Android by the Google Maps SDK built into the App, and on iPhone by Apple Maps (MapKit). When a map is shown, Google or Apple receives the map area (centred near you), device information (such as model and OS version), your IP address and diagnostic and usage data, handled under their privacy policies: Google (https://policies.google.com/privacy), Apple (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Neither the App nor the Developer receives this data.
  • Business directory (Taiwan): compiled from Ministry of Agriculture open data, shipped with the App and refreshed about monthly from this website (GitHub Pages). The download sends no personal data or location; GitHub sees only your IP address. If you choose an area by location, the location is blurred to about 100 m and sent to Nominatim to look up the city and district.
  • Ask AI: after you tap and confirm, the App copies a prepared summary (pet name, kind, breed, age, sex, weight, allergies, current medication, the last 30 days of health records and the symptoms you describe) to the clipboard and opens the AI service you choose; you paste it yourself. No phone numbers, addresses or photos are included. The AI service's own privacy policy applies.
  • Calls, directions, links: open your phone's dialler, maps or browser.
  • Sharing and export: backups, PDFs, recap pictures, memory cards and CSVs leave the phone only when you share them, to whoever you choose. Backups contain your data, photos, documents and phone numbers and are encrypted with your password (.petpal) by default; an unencrypted .zip is available but not recommended.
  • The PetPal mark on shared items: shared PDFs and pictures carry one small line with this website's address by default (text only on the lost-pet poster); you can turn it off in Settings. The link holds none of your data, and nothing tracks who opens it. "Invite family" and "Recommend to a friend" messages are sent by you through an app you choose and never include the family sync password; there are no referral rewards.
  • Family sync: sync packages leave the phone only when you share them through an app you choose (for example LINE). The App has no server, so the Developer never receives them. Packages are always encrypted with the family sync password (XChaCha20-Poly1305 and scrypt), which is kept only in the phone's secure storage. Private diary entries and this phone's settings are not included.
  • Rating invite: after a while the App may show a one-time invitation to send feedback; once the App is in the store it may also offer a rating. The rating window is shown and handled by Google Play or the App Store under their policies; the App never receives your rating, review or account details.
  • Feedback: opens your email app and you send it yourself; besides what you write and the screenshots you choose, it includes only the App version, OS version and interface language.
  • Phone backups: the Android version opts out of Google's automatic backup. iCloud or computer backups on iPhone may include the App's files; the data file stays encrypted and its key is not transferred.

5-1. Ads (Google AdMob)

Unless you buy “Remove ads”, a small ad from Google AdMob appears at the bottom of the app’s screens; emergency, first-aid, toxic-lookup, lost-pet, symptom-check, terms and editing screens never show ads. When an ad is shown, Google receives a device identifier (advertising ID), your IP address, ad views and taps, device information and diagnostics, handled under Google’s policies (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

On iPhone the app explains and then asks whether to allow tracking; if you don’t, the ads aren’t chosen by your interests. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see a consent form first and can change their choice later in Settings → Remove ads → Privacy options (ads). PetPal never gives your pet data, records or photos to advertisers and has no analytics of its own.

5-2. Remove ads (one-time purchase)

Payment for “Remove ads” is handled by the App Store or Google Play. PetPal never receives your payment details or account, only that this account owns the purchase. After buying, the app no longer requests or shows ads.

6. Health information

The symptom check, first-aid steps and toxic-substance lookup are general reference information, not a diagnosis, and do not replace a veterinarian. In an emergency contact an animal hospital directly.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Data stays on your phone until you delete it, either with Settings → Erase all data or by uninstalling the App. Because we keep no copy, we cannot restore it for you; export a backup first if you want to keep it. Dose check-ins are kept for the last 90 days.

8. Children

The App is not designed for children under 13 and does not collect anyone's personal data on our servers.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above; significant changes will be mentioned in the App's release notes.
